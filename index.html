How it works

A simple workflow that matches how tryouts actually run.

Create a session

Start a new tryout session and get a clean place to capture observations.

Import or paste a roster

Bring in jersey numbers and names (CSV/Excel) so your notes match real players.

Record voice notes

Speak naturally: “player 12 great first touch and quick decisions.” Keep moving.

Review, rate, and build teams

After the field work, review per-player notes and finalize team selections.

Privacy-first

Tryout Assistant is built to work without sign-in and without tracking. Your tryout notes live on your device. Speech transcription is provided by Apple’s Speech framework (on-device when available).

  • No accounts required
  • No analytics or ad tracking
