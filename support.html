Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record voice notes during a tryout?

Tap the microphone button on the capture screen to start recording. Speak your observations and the app will automatically transcribe them. Example: “player 12 great first touch and quick decisions.” Tap again to stop recording.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. All your data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to external servers. Your data may be included in your device backups (iCloud or local) if you have those enabled.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. TryoutsAssistant is designed to work offline during tryouts. Speech recognition runs on-device when available.

Do I need to say “player” before the number?

No, but it can help. You can say “12 great first touch” or “player 12 great first touch.” Tip: for jersey numbers 1–11, saying “player 6 …” can reduce confusion with position talk (e.g., “as a six”).

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual players, sessions, or notes within the app. To remove all data, simply delete the app from your device.

What devices are supported?

TryoutsAssistant requires iOS 26.1 or later and works on iPhone and iPad.

How do I import a roster?

From the roster screen, choose Import and select a CSV file. The app can auto-detect common headers like jersey number, name, and parent contact info.

Can I export my notes?

Yes. You can export session notes for sharing or further analysis. Export options may include JSON for notes and CSV/TXT for team and communication workflows.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email us at the address above with a description of the issue, what you expected to happen, and the steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are always helpful!
