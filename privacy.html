Privacy Policy

TryoutsAssistant

Overview

TryoutsAssistant ("the App") is designed with your privacy as a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS application.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All data you create within the App, including:

  • Voice recordings and transcriptions
  • Player information and evaluations
  • Notes and comments
  • Tryout session data

...is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third parties.

On-Device Processing

TryoutsAssistant uses on-device machine learning for:

  • Speech-to-text transcription
  • AI-powered analysis and insights

All processing happens entirely on your device. No audio or text data is sent to external servers for processing.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share any information with third parties.

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete individual records at any time within the App
  • Deleting the App will remove all associated data from your device
  • Data may be included in your iCloud or local device backups if you have those enabled

Children's Privacy

TryoutsAssistant does not knowingly collect any information from children. The App is intended for use by coaches and evaluators managing sports tryouts.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
